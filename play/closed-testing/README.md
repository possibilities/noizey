# Noizey closed-test recruitment

Google requires this personal developer account to keep at least 12 testers
continuously opted in to a closed test for 14 days before it can apply for
production access. Recruit 22–25 genuine US Android volunteers so the test
survives dropouts and produces useful feedback. Do not begin the 14-day count
until the Console shows at least 12 testers opted in.

Official references:

- <https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465>
- <https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334>
- <https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6321495>

## Privacy and access

- Prefer a Google Group or a private Play tester list. Never ask volunteers to
  post their Google-account address publicly.
- A private, invite-only Google Group named `Noizey Closed Testers` is prepared
  for Play access. Only group managers can post or view the member list; add
  volunteers only after they consent through the signup flow.
- Two Google Forms are prepared in the operator's Drive. Find them by the exact
  titles `Noizey Android closed-test signup` and `Noizey closed-test feedback`.
  Keep responder URLs out of this public repository; share the signup form only
  in active recruitment and the feedback form only with enrolled testers.
- Store the tester roster outside this repository. Record only what is needed to
  manage opt-in continuity and feedback, and delete it when the test is complete.
- Closed testers normally must purchase paid apps. If Play Console enables
  paid-app promo codes for the closed release, issue one private one-time code
  per enrolled tester so the test copy is free. Never publish codes in a post
  or shared roster. Otherwise stop and resolve the cost before recruiting.
- Ask for candid private feedback, never a Play rating or positive review.

## No-cost recruitment order

1. Friends, family, and colleagues in the US who actually use sleep, focus, or
   sound-masking apps.
2. `r/alphaandbetausers`, requesting genuine Android beta feedback.
3. `r/AndroidClosedTesting`, offering reciprocal app testing without exchanging
   ratings or reviews and confirming each volunteer can access US-only apps.
4. `r/ProductivityApps` only after the posting account has the community's
   required karma, using its Self Promotion flair and a user-focused explanation.
5. One free reciprocal-testing service, such as BetaCircle or Closed Test Pro,
   only after confirming its current free terms, US eligibility, and that both
   sides will test genuinely without exchanging ratings or reviews.

Do not use paid tester farms, bots, purchased accounts, install exchanges, or
communities that prohibit self-promotion. Check the live rules immediately
before every post.

## Feedback evidence

Ask volunteers to try realistic flows:

- select, mix, and adjust several sounds;
- set and cancel the sleep timer;
- pause and resume from the lock screen and notification;
- use wired headphones and Bluetooth devices;
- observe playback through calls, notifications, and app switching;
- note battery use, accessibility, volume behavior, and unclear onboarding.

Retain a privacy-safe summary of use cases, devices, feedback themes, defects,
and resulting fixes. Google asks for this evidence in the production-access
application.
