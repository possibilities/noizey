# Noizey closed-test recruitment

Google requires this personal developer account to keep at least 12 testers
continuously opted in to a closed test for 14 days before it can apply for
production access. Target 18–20 volunteers so the test survives dropouts and
produces useful feedback.

Official references:

- <https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465>
- <https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334>
- <https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6321495>

## Privacy and access

- Prefer a Google Group or a private Play tester list. Never ask volunteers to
  post their Google-account address publicly.
- Store the tester roster outside this repository. Record only what is needed to
  manage opt-in continuity and feedback, and delete it when the test is complete.
- Closed testers normally must purchase paid apps. If Play Console enables
  paid-app promo codes for the closed release, use one-time codes to make the
  test copy free. Otherwise stop and resolve the cost before recruiting.
- Ask for candid private feedback, never a Play rating or positive review.

## No-cost recruitment order

1. Friends, family, and colleagues who actually use sleep, focus, or sound-masking apps.
2. `r/ProductivityApps`, with a user-focused explanation rather than a quota request.
3. `r/alphaandbetausers`, requesting genuine Android beta feedback.
4. `r/AndroidClosedTesting`, offering reciprocal app testing without exchanging reviews.
5. One free credit-based exchange, such as BetaCircle, only after confirming its
   current free terms and testing other developers' apps genuinely.

Do not use paid tester farms, bots, purchased accounts, install exchanges, or
communities that prohibit self-promotion. Check the live rules immediately
before every post.

## Outreach draft

> **[Android][Closed beta] Free copy of Noizey — looking for genuine sleep and focus testers for 14 days**
>
> Noizey is an independent Android ambient-sound app for sleep, focus, noisy
> environments, and sound masking. I am looking for Android users who will opt
> in through Google Play, keep the test active for 14 days, and give candid
> private feedback on sound mixing, timers, background playback, battery use,
> Bluetooth/headphones, interruptions, and onboarding.
>
> No rating or positive review is requested; critical feedback is welcome. I
> will provide a free paid-app promo code if Google Play enables them for this
> closed release. Comment or DM if interested so no Google-account address is
> posted publicly.

For reciprocal-testing communities, add one sentence offering to test another
developer's app genuinely. Never promise a rating or review.

## Feedback evidence

Ask volunteers to try realistic flows:

- select, mix, and adjust several sounds;
- set and cancel the sleep timer;
- pause and resume from the lock screen and notification;
- use wired headphones and Bluetooth devices;
- observe playback through calls, notifications, and app switching;
- note battery use, accessibility, volume behavior, and unclear onboarding.

Retain a privacy-safe summary of use cases, devices, feedback themes, defects,
and resulting fixes. Google asks for this evidence in the production-access
application.
